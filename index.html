Why Serie Beauty

Built on three things a full-range brand can't promise.

Priced for a student's budget, not a mall counter

Carrying no shopfront, staff or rental cost lets Serie Beauty sit consistently below halal competitors like Sobella, without cutting the formulation quality shoppers are paying for.

JAKIM halal-certified, from formula to factory

Every product is made under Malaysian Standard MS 2634:2019 at a JAKIM-audited OEM, so the badge on the product page reflects the full supply chain, not just the finished bottle.

The same shelf from Kuala Lumpur to Kota Kinabalu

Selling only through Shopee and TikTok Shop means no customer is disadvantaged for living outside the Klang Valley — every shopper reaches the same six shades on the same terms.

"With no physical shopfront, packaging and video content serve as the brand's only store."

Serie Beauty carries no rental, retail staff or shopfront fit-out costs — every sale runs through its official storefronts on Shopee and TikTok Shop, supported by Instagram and TikTok for content and community. That leaner cost base is what allows the brand to price below comparable halal-certified competitors while keeping a soft pink, minimalist identity across banners, product photography and unboxing.

Rather than building a full face, lips, eyes and cheeks range, Serie Beauty stays deliberately narrow: one lipstick, one blusher, three shades each. It's a considered choice for a first-year online start-up — lower production risk, a focused "two-step face" story to market, and a range a small founding team can actually shoot, restock and sell without spreading thin. As demand proves out across Malaysia, the assortment widens on purpose, not by accident.

Halal Promise

Certified across the whole chain, not just the finished bottle.

Every Serie Beauty product is manufactured under JAKIM halal certification in line with Malaysian Standard MS 2634:2019, which assesses raw material sourcing, production, hygiene and storage — not only what ends up in the tube. Both hero products are also notified to the National Pharmaceutical Regulatory Agency before sale, managed by the manufacturer as part of the sourcing agreement.
